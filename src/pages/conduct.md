## Who this applies to

Everyone in a mukto.net space: on the server, in chat, on the git forge, at events, and anywhere you represent mukto.net.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are learning. Everyone was a beginner once, including at the thing you're best at.
- **Welcome people as they are.** Gender, religion, ethnicity, language, age, disability, sexuality, where you studied, which city you're from, whether you code in Bangla or English: none of it decides who belongs.
- **Argue about the work.** Criticise code, designs and plans as hard as you like. Leave the person out of it.
- **Share what you know.** Answer the question you wish someone had answered for you.
- **Respect shared resources.** Other people live on this machine too.
- **Ask before you share.** Other people's code, words, photos and personal details are theirs.

## What isn't allowed

- Harassment, threats, insults and deliberate intimidation, online or at events.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, comments or imagery.
- Hate speech, or attacks on anyone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Following, recording or photographing people at events after they've asked you not to.
- Encouraging or helping anyone do any of the above.

## If something happens

Tell an event organiser in person, message anyone in the conduct group, or email conduct@mukto.net. You'll get a reply from someone who isn't involved. We'll keep your report confidential, and we won't share your name with the person you report without your permission.

## What admins can do

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public warning, removal from a chat or an event, suspension of an account, or a permanent ban from mukto.net spaces. Admins and committee members are held to this code too. The conduct group answers to the general assembly, not the committee, so a complaint about a committee member is handled by people that member doesn't oversee.
