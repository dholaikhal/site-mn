## Who can have an account

Anyone 16 or older, or younger with a guardian's written consent. One account per person. You agree to the [code of conduct](/conduct/) and the [acceptable use policy](/acceptable-use/), which are part of these terms.

## What you get

A shell account, a web page, git hosting and whatever else the [services page](/services/) lists as available to you. It's free. Resources are shared, so there are disk and process limits, and heavier services are granted on request.

## What we promise, and what we don't

We run the services as well as volunteers can. We don't promise they'll always be up, fast or free of bugs, and we don't promise to keep any service forever. If we shut one down, we give at least 60 days' notice, except in an emergency.

We take daily backups, but don't rely on them. Keep your own copy of anything you care about.

## Your content

What you make on mukto.net is yours. You're responsible for it, and for having the right to publish it. By putting something in `~/public_html` or a public repository, you let us store and serve it. We claim no other rights to it.

## Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time by running `mukto-leave` or emailing hello@mukto.net. We delete your home directory and repositories within 30 days.

We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, the code of conduct or the acceptable use policy. Except when we have to act immediately to protect the server or other people, we'll tell you why first and give you a chance to respond. You can appeal to the general assembly.
