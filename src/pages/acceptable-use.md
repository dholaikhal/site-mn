## What mukto.net is for

Learning, hobby projects, community projects and open-source work. It isn't production hosting for a business, and it isn't anonymous hosting.

## Not allowed

- **Anything illegal** in Bangladesh or in the country where the server is hosted.
- **Attacking anything**: scanning, brute-forcing, exploiting or denial-of-service against any system, ours or anyone else's. Security learning happens on systems you own or in labs built for it.
- **Spam**: bulk or unsolicited email, comment spam, SEO spam.
- **Cryptocurrency mining**, and any other job that burns shared CPU for profit.
- **Proxies and VPN exits** open to people who aren't mukto.net members, and Tor exit nodes.
- **Phishing pages, malware, and pirated software or media.**
- **Bots that hammer other sites** or ignore their terms of service.
- **Sharing your account.** Accounts are personal. Make one per person.

## Be a good neighbour

- Keep long-running processes small, and ask before running anything that uses lots of CPU, memory or disk for long.
- Stay within your disk quota. Clean up after experiments.
- Background services and listening ports need an admin's OK.

## Privacy, and what admins can see

Admins can see process lists, resource usage and file sizes, as on any shared Unix system. They won't read your files or messages unless they have to investigate a breach of this policy or the code of conduct, or the law requires it, and when that happens it's logged and the committee is told.

## What happens if you break this

Usually a message asking you to stop. For anything harmful, or when it keeps happening, the process can be killed, the service disabled or the account suspended, without warning if necessary to protect the server or other people.
