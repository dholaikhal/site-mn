## 1. Name and nature

mukto.net (মুক্ত) is a not-for-profit, volunteer-run association of people who use and build free and open-source technology in Bangladesh. It has no owners and pays no dividends. Nobody is paid to run it.

## 2. Purpose

- Run shared computing services for members: shell accounts, web pages, git hosting and more, using only free and open-source software.
- Teach technical skills, in Bangla and English, free of charge.
- Contribute to free and open-source software, including its Bangla localisation.
- Hold events open to the public.

## 3. Members

- Anyone 16 or older who agrees to the [code of conduct](/conduct/) and the [acceptable use policy](/acceptable-use/) can be a member. Under-16s can join with a guardian's written consent.
- There is no membership fee. Contributions are voluntary and don't buy votes.
- Every member has one vote in the general assembly.
- Membership ends on resignation, after 12 months without a login and without replying to two emails, or by decision of the conduct group under the code of conduct.

## 4. General assembly

- The general assembly of all members is the highest authority. It meets once a year, in December, and whenever the committee or one fifth of members call it.
- Notice: 21 days, with the agenda. Members can vote in person or online.
- Quorum: one fifth of members.
- It elects the executive committee, adopts the budget and the accounts, and amends this constitution.

## 5. Executive committee

- Ten members, elected each year by the general assembly: convener, general secretary, treasurer, and seven others. Nobody serves more than three consecutive terms in the same role.
- It runs mukto.net between assemblies, meets at least monthly and publishes minutes in the [logbook](/logbook/) within 14 days.
- Decisions are by consensus where possible, otherwise by simple majority; quorum is six.
- Any spending over ৳10,000 needs two officers' approval, and anything over ৳50,000 needs the general assembly.

## 6. Working groups

The committee can create or close working groups. Any member can join one. The conduct group is the exception: its members are elected by the general assembly and it reports to the assembly, not the committee.

## 7. Conflicts of interest

Anyone with a personal or business interest in a decision says so and doesn't vote on it. This is recorded in the minutes.

## 8. Money

- The treasurer records all income and spending in a ledger, with a receipt for every expense. Any member can inspect the ledger and receipts on request.
- A summary is published every quarter. See [accounts](/accounts/).
- The annual accounts are reviewed by two members who don't sit on the committee and presented to the general assembly.
- Money and hardware may only be used for the purposes in article 2.

## 9. Amendments

A two-thirds majority of members voting at a general assembly, with the proposed text circulated 21 days in advance.

## 10. Dissolution

A two-thirds majority at a general assembly called for the purpose. After debts are paid, any remaining money and hardware go to another not-for-profit with similar purposes, chosen by that assembly, and never to members.
