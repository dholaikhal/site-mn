## How to report

Email **security@mukto.net**. Encrypt it with ~kalbaishakhi's PGP key if it's sensitive (fingerprint on the [team page](/team/)). Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is.

We reply within three days, fix serious issues as fast as volunteers can, and tell you when it's fixed.

## In scope

- The shared server, `mukto.net` and its web pages
- The git forge, `git.mukto.net`
- Chat, `irc.mukto.net`
- This website and its join form

## Out of scope

- Denial-of-service, spam and social engineering of members or volunteers
- Other members' `~/public_html` pages and projects. Report those to the member
- Anything that needs you to read or change another member's data to prove it

## Safe harbour

If you test in good faith, stay in scope, don't access other people's data and give us time to fix things before you publish, we won't complain to anyone about your research. Testing from a member account is fine. Testing against members isn't.

## Thanks

There's no bug bounty: we don't have the money. People who report real issues are thanked here, with their permission.
