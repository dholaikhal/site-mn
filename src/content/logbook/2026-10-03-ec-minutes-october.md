**Present:** farhana (chair), tanvir (minutes), arpita, shutki_daemon, kalbaishakhi, sabbir, nusrat, prottoy, labiba. **Apologies:** bdix_bhai. Quorum met (9 of 10). Online, 21:00–22:35.

**1. Minutes of 4 September** approved without changes.

**2. Treasurer's report.** Received. Contributions came from fewer people in September than in August. Arpita will post the Q3 summary in the logbook. Noted.

**3. Install-fest, Rajshahi, 5 December.** Tahsin and rajshahi_aam have a lab confirmed. Budget up to ৳8,000 for USB drives and lunch. *Approved, unanimous.*

**4. Annual general meeting.** Fixed for Saturday 19 December, 15:00, in Dhaka with remote voting. Notice goes out by 28 November with the draft constitution. Nominations for the committee open the same day. *Approved, unanimous.*

**5. Disk quota.** Shutki_daemon proposed raising it from 2 GB to 5 GB: 40 of 49 users are under 500 MB, and the disk is 31% full. *Approved, unanimous.*

**6. BDIX mirror.** Bdix_bhai (by message) has two ISPs interested in hosting a Debian and Ubuntu mirror in exchange for a credit on the site. Kalbaishakhi asked who would have root on that machine. Long discussion. Agreed: no hardware we don't administer ourselves, and a credit no bigger than any other supporter's. Bdix_bhai to go back to both with those terms. *Approved, 8 for, 1 abstention (labiba, absent for part of the item).*

**7. Conduct.** Nusrat reported one complaint in September, resolved with a private warning. No names. Noted.

**8. Any other business.** Sabbir asked for a second projector. Deferred to November.

Next meeting: Friday 6 November, 21:00.
